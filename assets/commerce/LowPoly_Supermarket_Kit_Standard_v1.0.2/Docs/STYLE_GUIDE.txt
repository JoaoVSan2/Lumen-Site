AMANO PROPS - STYLE GUIDE (Supermarket Kit v1.0.2)
=======================================================

1. SCALE
   - 1 unit = 1 meter. Supermarket walls 4 m wide x 5 m high (parapet included), roof top 4.5 m, ceiling 4.2-4.3 m,
     sliding door 2.6 x 3.3 m, aisles 1.9 m high, checkout counter 0.88 m, loading dock platform 1.2 m.
2. GRID
   - 4 m grid: walls, storefront modules, floor / roof / ceiling tiles, parking tiles, rack bays
     (= Town Starter road tiles = Gas Station 24h canopy cells = Terrain Kit tiles).
   - 2 m grid: aisles (gondolas), open chillers, freezers, counters. End caps close a row.
3. ORIGINS AND ORIENTATION
   - Origin on the ground at the center of the footprint; front faces -Y in Blender.
   - Walls: origin at the foot of the wall on the grid line, outside = -Y.
   - Wall items: back on the wall plane. Ceiling and hanging items: origin on the ceiling. Moving parts: separate
     child objects, pivot on the hinge / hub / track.
4. PALETTES
   - ONE texture: Textures/props_palette.png, 16 x 16 cells of 16 px (256 x 256 px).
   - Every polygon is UV-mapped to the CENTER of one cell: no texture painting.
   - Rows 0-7 are identical to Town Starter MegaKit, rows 0-10 to Route 66 Desert Stop, rows 0-12 (first half) to
     Gas Station 24h Kit; the supermarket colors fill the free cells (row 12 second half, row 13, row 14).
     v1.0.2: cells 237-249 = the Diner Kit colors (identical), cells 250-254 = sky dome colors; cell 255 is free.
     Colors are only ever ADDED.
   - Classic (day), Night (dark blue, windows and neons lit), Pastel, Winter: same UVs, swap the palette AND the
     matching emission map.
   - Glass uses a second material, PropsGlass (plain color, alpha 0.2), the same in every palette.
5. STYLE
   - Low poly, flat shaded; 54 to 2,222 polygons (quads) per asset (design limit: 2,500 polygons).
   - Objects only: no characters, no real brands. Products are plain colored packs without labels; signs use
     generic pictograms; the only word is the generic MARKET on Mart_Sign_Neon_Market_6m.
6. NAMING
   - Family_Variant (Mart_Wall_Door_Slide_4m, Aisle_Shelf_Cans_2m, Checkout_Lane_Express_Market...). Names never
     collide with the other Amano Props packs. Existing file names, scales and pivots never change in updates.
