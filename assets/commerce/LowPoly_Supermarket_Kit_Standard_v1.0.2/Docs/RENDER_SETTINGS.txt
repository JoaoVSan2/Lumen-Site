LOW POLY SUPERMARKET KIT v1.0.2 - RENDER SETTINGS (sky, lights, fog, exposure, glow of the images)
==================================================================================================
Everything you see in the images and demo scenes is a model of the pack (ground, sky dome, neon halos, display
bases). Only the light, fog, exposure and glow are engine settings: here are all their values, so you can rebuild
the same look. (Blender units: watts; Godot: energy; Unreal: candelas / lux, see the Unreal README.)

1. SKY
   Day:   Sky_Dome_Day_Market  (horizon #CFE2F2 -> blue_light #8AB8E0 -> zenith #6FA6E0), unlit / unshaded.
   Night: Sky_Dome_Night_Market (horizon #2F3C59 -> #1C2742 -> zenith #0B1428), unlit / unshaded.
   Dome at the origin of the scene, radius 190 m: camera far plane 450 m or more. No shadow, no fog on the dome.

2. BLENDER (Cycles, view transform "Standard", exposure 0)
   Day:   world color (0.52, 0.68, 0.95) strength 1.0; sun "Key" 3.0 W/m2 color (1.0, 0.93, 0.82) from (0.55, 0.8, -1.0)
          + sun "Fill" 1.0 color (0.78, 0.86, 1.0) from (-0.8, 0.3, -0.4).
   Night: world (lighting only) (0.0025, 0.0035, 0.008); moon = the two suns x 0.02, color (0.45, 0.55, 1.0);
          palette emission strength 5.0 (neons, LED, screens; 1.3 by day);
          parking lamps: 2 spots per Lamp_Parking_Double_Flood, 11000 W, color (1.0, 0.80, 0.52), cone 58 deg,
          blend 0.45, 8.66 m high, tilted 7 deg outward;
          storefront ceilings: area 3.2 x 5.0 m, 190 W, (1.0, 0.95, 0.86); window spill: area 3.3 x 2.0 m, 58 W,
          (1.0, 0.92, 0.78); entrance spill 100 W; awning spots 75 W (1.0, 0.86, 0.66) cone 85 deg;
          entrance pergola spots 230 W cone 95 deg; wall packs 320 W cone 110 deg; fridges 70 W (0.82, 0.92, 1.0);
          MARKET sign glow on the brick: point 260 W (1.0, 0.22, 0.14) radius 1.2 m + 2 x 117 W.
   Neon halos: material PropsHalo = palette color, emission strength 1.0, alpha 0.18.
   Samples 40 (day) / 64 (night), denoising on. No post-process glow in the v1.0.2 images (the halos are models).

3. GODOT 4.7 (Godot_Demo/, Pro)
   One Environment file per scene: Godot_Demo/environments/env_<scene>.tres
     background = sky dome horizon color, ambient light (day 0.78/0.82/0.90 x 0.6, night 0.30/0.36/0.60 x 0.35),
     tonemap Linear, exposure 1.0, SSAO radius 1.2 intensity 1.5 (1.2 indoors),
     glow intensity 0.4 (day) / 0.9 (night), strength 1.0, bloom 0.0 / 0.05, blend Additive, HDR threshold 1.0 / 0.9,
     fog (night only): density 0.006 (supermarket_dense) / 0.01 (market_night_psx), color = 2 x night sky color.
   Sun: DirectionalLight3D energy 1.25 color (1.0, 0.95, 0.86) by day; 0.12 color (0.5, 0.6, 1.0) at night.
   Lamps: SpotLight3D energy = Blender W / 1600 (max 7), range 16 m, angle = cone / 2 + 6; area lights ->
     OmniLight3D energy = W / 160 (max 2), range 7 m; point lights -> OmniLight3D energy = W / 70 (max 3).
   Palette emission energy: 1.3 by day, 2.0 at night (demo_setup.gd > emission_energy).
   prefabs/<Model>_Lit.tscn: model + halo + light, ready to use.

4. UNREAL 5.8 (Unreal_UE5.8_Project/, Pro) - full list in Unreal_UE5.8_Project/README.txt. Summary:
   Night: moon 1 lux (0.55, 0.65, 1.0); Sky Light 0.4; height fog 0.004 (volumetric, start 15 m, max opacity 0.6);
          manual exposure +0.3, bloom 0.5; lamps = Godot energy x 45 cd (spots) / x 8 cd (points).
   Day:   sun 60000 lux (1.0, 0.93, 0.82), pitch -40, yaw 230; Sky Light 1.0; fog 0.004; exposure -13, bloom 0.4.
   Interior: no fog; exposure -2.2; 15 rect lights 3 x 3 m, 30 cd, (1.0, 0.98, 0.95) under the LED ceiling panels.
   Sky domes: M_Sky unlit, MI_Sky_Night x1.0, MI_Sky_Day x5800 (because of the day exposure -13).
   Halos: M_Halo additive unlit, MI_Halo strength 0.6 x opacity 0.18. MARKET sign: MI_Props_Sign (emissive 0.5),
          its 3 glow lights on the brick x 0.25, color (1.0, 0.1, 0.08) (the sign stays red, not orange).
